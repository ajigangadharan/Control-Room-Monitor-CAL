    Each row is one sequence tile on the wall. Each column is one set. Camera numbers carry on from the last critical camera. The highlighted column is the set now playing.
    Clause references are to SIRA Administrative Resolution No. 13 of 2025, Part 1, Chapter 1, "Third: Display devices" unless stated otherwise. Page numbers are ±1 from the English text. PASS/FAIL rows are mandatory SIRA rows. REVIEW/OK rows are advisory. OPT rows are stricter than the text.

    Calculation basis

    • Critical monitors = ceil(critical cameras ÷ tiles per monitor). Critical cameras are static and must sit at the centre of the wall (item 9).
    • Sequence tiles = ceil(normal cameras ÷ sets). Sequence monitors = ceil(sequence tiles ÷ tiles per monitor). The auto layout splits them between the left and right edges (item 10, "terminal areas").
    • Sets used = ceil(normal cameras ÷ sequence tiles installed), never more than the sets entered. Dwell = cycle time ÷ sets used.
    • Camera numbering: C1 to C(critical) are the static cameras. Sequence cameras continue from C(critical + 1). Set 1 holds the first block of normal cameras, set 2 the next block, and so on. Camera content follows a monitor when you drag it.
    • Tile image size = active area ÷ split, unrounded. If the camera aspect ratio differs from the tile, the image is fitted inside the tile and the fitted image is checked against 120 × 67 mm.
    • Custom layout: the wall grid is the bounding box of the placed monitors. Empty slots inside it count towards wall width and height (blank panel or filler). The centre check passes when no sequence monitor is closer to the wall's centre line than a critical monitor.

    Space requirement basis

    • Outer panel size = active area + 2 × bezel per edge (standard sizes), or the outer size you enter (manual).
    • Display width = columns × outer width + (columns − 1) × extra gap. Display height likewise with rows.
    • Top of wall above FFL = FFL to bottom of lowest row + display height. Ceiling needed = top of wall + clearance above.
    • Overall width = display width + 2 × side clearance. Depth = panel and mount depth + rear service zone.
    • Clearance defaults (300 mm above, 300 mm each side) are planning placeholders, not a SIRA or ISO figure. Replace them with the manufacturer's and your reference's values.

    How the SIRA rules are read here

    • The 12 × 6.7 cm minimum is in the Remarks of item 5, the wall monitor row. This tool applies it to the video wall. It is not clearly required for the 21" workstation monitors (item 8). Tick the option on the Operators tab to apply it there too, as a stricter design choice.
    • Item 11 limits "the thickness of the outer frame of wall monitors" to 4 mm. This tool reads that as the bezel on each edge. If SIRA reads it as the bezel-to-bezel gap, the limit is effectively 2 mm per edge.
    • Sequencing: at most 6 sets, full cycle at most 60 s. The law sets no minimum dwell and no camera-to-tile ratio.
    • Item 7 remark: sites with no operations room and fewer than 64 cameras or fewer than 4 recorders may use one screen with a KVM. The "or" probably means "and"; the Arabic text decides.
    • The manual has no operator-to-camera ratio, no viewing-distance rule and no wall clearance rule. Ergonomic and space rows are advisory only.
